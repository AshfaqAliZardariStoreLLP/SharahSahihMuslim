Privacy Policy

Sharah Sahih Muslim • Ashfaq Ali Zardari Store, LLP

Last Updated: October 27, 2026

1. Introduction

Ashfaq Ali Zardari Store, LLP ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with the Sharah Sahih Muslim mobile application (the "App") and our website at www.ashfaqalizardaristore.co (the "Website").

By downloading and using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The Sharah Sahih Muslim App is designed with a privacy-first, offline architecture. We do not collect, store on our servers, transmit, sell, or share any personal data from your device.

Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address.
  • Your location data (GPS or network-based).
  • Your device identifiers (IMEI, MAC address, advertising ID).
  • Your contacts, photos, files, or calendar information.
  • Your usage analytics or behavioral data.

No account registration is required to use the App.

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

To provide the reading experience, the App stores the following information only on your device, using a local SQLite database (Drift). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us:

  • Which volumes you have downloaded.
  • Your last-read page number in each volume.
  • Your bookmarked or favorited passages.
  • The local file path and size of downloaded PDF files.

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

4. File Downloads from Google Drive

The App downloads PDF volumes from publicly shared links hosted on Google Drive. When you initiate a download, your device connects directly to Google's servers. Google's own privacy policy governs any data Google processes during that connection. We do not receive or store any information about your download activity.

5. PDF Export and Sharing

The App allows you to export or share downloaded PDF files using your device's native share functionality. When you choose to share a file (for example, via email, messaging, or cloud storage), the recipient and the third-party service you select will handle the file according to their own privacy policies.

6. Permissions Used by the App

The App requests the following Android permissions only to perform its core functions:

  • Internet: To download volume PDFs from Google Drive.
  • Network State: To detect whether a connection is available before starting a download.
  • Storage (on older Android versions): To save downloaded PDF files within the App's private storage area.

The App does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, or any other sensitive permission.

7. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Because no personal data is collected, the App is safe for use by all ages. If you believe that a child's personal information has been inadvertently transmitted to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. International Data Protection Compliance

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal data, it is designed to respect privacy laws globally. Our approach reflects the principles of:

  • GDPR (European Union): We do not process personal data of EU residents through the App. We nonetheless provide this Policy for transparency and to meet app-store requirements, as recommended for apps that collect no data[reference:2].
  • Pakistan Personal Data Protection Bill 2023: We do not process personal data as defined under the draft Bill through the App[reference:3].
  • Google Play Data Safety requirements: We have completed the Data Safety declaration in Google Play Console to state that no data is collected or shared, consistent with Google's mandatory form for all apps[reference:4].

9. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate third-party analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking libraries. The only external service contacted is Google Drive, solely to download the volume files you request.

10. Data Security

Since the App does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no central database of user information that could be breached. Data stored locally on your device is protected by your device's own security mechanisms (screen lock, encryption, and app sandboxing).

11. Your Rights

Because we do not hold any personal data about you, there is no personal information for us to access, correct, delete, or port. You have full control over all app data stored on your device and can remove it at any time by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will post the updated policy on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
